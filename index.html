Dance Again!

Restoring mobility through non-invasive, head-controlled assistive technology.

The Mission

We bridge the gap between human intention and mechanical movement. By leveraging advanced EEG signal processing and upper-body tracking, Dance Again! empowers individuals to control robotic exoskeletons seamlessly and non-invasively.

The Technology

Our framework interprets real-time neural telemetry and ergonomic motion sensors. This translates user gestures into fluid, precise mechanical control commands, completely bypassing traditional physical interfaces.
